# OTT Customer Churn Analysis 📊

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn in an OTT subscription platform like Netflix, Hotstar, and Amazon Prime. The objective is to identify customers at high risk of leaving, understand churn patterns, analyze revenue loss, and provide actionable insights to improve customer retention.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Identify high-risk customers likely to cancel subscriptions.
- Analyze churn based on subscription plans and contract types.
- Understand customer demographics and subscription behavior.
- Evaluate the impact of customer complaints and support escalations.
- Measure revenue loss and customer lifetime value (CLTV).
- Recommend strategies to improve customer retention.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **Python:** Data analysis and processing
- **SQL:** Data extraction and querying
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and cleaning
- **NumPy:** Numerical operations
- **SQLite3:** Database connectivity

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Database Connection:** Connected Python with SQLite database to extract customer information from multiple relational tables.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, corrected data types, renamed columns, and removed unnecessary data.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Calculated customer tenure, churn rates, retention rates, and customer aging.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analyzed churn patterns using grouping, aggregation, and pivot tables.
5. **Data Visualization:** Created charts using Matplotlib and Seaborn to identify important trends.
6. **Business Insights:** Identified churn patterns, revenue risks, and opportunities to improve customer retention.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Customer Churn Rate
- Customer Retention Rate
- Churn by Subscription Plan
- Churn by State
- Average Customer Tenure
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Revenue at Risk
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
- Support Escalation Rate

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Overall customer churn rate: **28.6%**
- Customer retention rate: **71.4%**
- Monthly subscription churn: **55.6%**
- Annual subscription churn: **8.3%**
- Average customer tenure: **1,451 days**
- Revenue loss due to churn: **74**
- CLTV lost: **2,047**

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Focus on customers with medium and high churn risk.
- Investigate customer complaints and support escalations.
- Encourage monthly subscribers to migrate to annual plans.
- Analyze pricing and subscription issues in high-churn regions.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns through email, SMS, and calls.
